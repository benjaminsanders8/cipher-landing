Questions

Answers, as promised

What is Cipher?

Cipher is a healthcare benefits intelligence platform for self-insured employers and the consultants, brokers, and TPAs who serve them. It runs inside your environment, turns medical and pharmacy claims into patient-level intelligence, and delivers decisions: benchmarked spend, forecasts, vendor validation, and recommended actions sized in dollars — packaged in a board-ready CFO Summary.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere. Cipher deploys inside your environment and reads your medical and pharmacy claims where they already live — no migration, no third-party warehouse. If a vendor holds your data today, we build the feed; if access is blocked, we help unlock it.

Do we need an analytics team to use Cipher?

No. Cipher is built for finance and HR leaders. We do the analysis; you get the story and the action list.

Does Cipher replace our broker or consultant?

No — it makes their work sharper. Many advisors bring Cipher to their clients. If you have a broker you trust, we're happy to work alongside them.

We're fully insured — is Cipher for us?

Cipher is designed for self-insured (and level-funded) employers, where every claim dollar is your dollar. If you're weighing a move to self-funding, we can help you evaluate it.
